👩‍🌾 About Us

Hello!

My name is Viktoria Viktorovna Nikolenko. Every day I work to ensure that residents of Kurganinsk can purchase fresh natural milk directly from the producer.

We sell fresh whole milk from the evening milking, cooled to +4°C. Proper cooling preserves its freshness, taste, and beneficial properties.

🥛 Our Milk

Our milk comes from Russian Black-and-White dairy cows and Dutch Jersey cows.

The milk fat content ranges from 3.6% to 4.7%.

The milk is sold whole and is perfect for making:

  • Matsoni
  • Cottage Cheese
  • Adyghe Cheese
  • Homemade Sour Cream

Even during the summer heat, the milk remains fresh until you arrive home.

🌽 Feeding Our Cows

Our cows receive a balanced diet.

Their feed includes:

  • Corn
  • Silage
  • High-quality feed mixtures
